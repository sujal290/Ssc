🧠 Accuracy Notes from the Source

Prithviraj Raso: traditionally attributed to Chand Bardai and semi-legendary; Prithvirajavijaya is presented as the better near-contemporary literary source.
Kavirajamarga: traditionally associated with Amoghavarsha I, while court-poet attribution is also discussed.
Guru Granth Sahib / Adi Granth: Guru Arjan compiled the Adi Granth; Guru Gobind Singh later completed the scripture tradition.
Padshahnama: multiple versions are associated with Abdul Hamid Lahori and Muhammad Amin Qazvini.
An Era of Darkness: the standard title of Shashi Tharoor's 2016 work.
Periodical trap: Young India, Harijan, Navajivan and Bahishkrit Bharat are periodicals/publications, not books.
