PVTG — MUST KNOW

75 PVTG communities are covered under the current Government framework.
  • Andaman: Great Andamanese, Jarawa, Onge, Sentinelese, Shompen
  • Jharkhand: Asur, Birhor, Birjia, Korwa, Mal Paharia, Sauria Paharia
  • Odisha: Bonda, Dongria Kondh, Juang, Kutia Kondh, Didayi, Saora
  • MP/CG: Baiga, Bharia, Kamar, Sahariya, Abujh Maria, Hill Korwa
  • South: Kattunayakan, Toda, Kota, Irular, Paniyan, Koraga
