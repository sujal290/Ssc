SCO — Basic Facts

Foundation

What is SCO?

A Eurasian political, economic and security alliance. The uploaded material describes it as the world's largest regional organisation in terms of geographical coverage and population.

Established

15 June 2001. Headquarters: Beijing, China.

Shanghai Five

The SCO originated as the Shanghai Five in 1996 to address regional security and border-related concerns.

Expansion

India and Pakistan became full members in 2017. Iran joined in 2023 and Belarus joined in 2024.

26th SCO Summit — 2026

Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan
31 Aug–1 Sep
The 26th SCO Summit took place in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, marking the 25th anniversary of the bloc.
Theme
“25 Years of the SCO: Together for Sustainable Peace, Development and Prosperity”
Host
Kyrgyz President Sadyr Japarov, closing Kyrgyzstan's 2025–2026 rotating chairmanship.
2026–27
Pakistan assumed the SCO chairmanship for the 2026–27 term. Its theme: “Turning Vision into Action: Connectivity, Innovation and Shared Prosperity”.
Tourism
Lahore received the status of SCO Tourism and Cultural Capital for 2026–2027.

India & PM Modi's Address

Exam Focus

Three-Pillar Framework

Security, Connectivity and Opportunity were highlighted by Prime Minister Narendra Modi at the summit.

Terrorism

He called for dismantling the ecosystem of terror financing, radicalization and safe havens, and stated that terrorism can never be a strategic asset for any state.

Afghanistan

India's humanitarian aid to Afghanistan was highlighted, along with the link between Eurasian peace and Afghanistan's stability.

Kabul

The address urged for an inclusive government in Kabul.

Bishkek Declaration

Key outcomes
  • Covered multilateral cooperation in politics, security, trade, investment, energy, industry, ecology, tourism and new technologies.
  • Leaders adopted the Bishkek Declaration along with four MoUs and over twenty decisions.
  • Areas included climate action, energy conservation, nuclear safety and digital cooperation.
  • The declaration addressed counter-terrorism and the “three evils”: terrorism, separatism and extremism.
  • The uploaded material records positions on Iran and West Asia, including support for diplomatic resolutions and Iran's peaceful nuclear programme under the NPT.
  • English was approved for official/working-language recognition within the SCO Charter framework, alongside the earlier Russian and Chinese framework described in the material.

Major Initiatives & Agreements

2026

🛡️ Cybersecurity

2026–2028 International Information Security Cooperation Plan to coordinate responses to cyber threats and digital sabotage.

💊 Anti-Narcotics

Stricter regulatory frameworks for the SCO Anti-Narcotics Centre, which the material says will operate out of Dushanbe, targeting cross-border trafficking and synthetic drugs through 2030.

🌱 Green Technology

Green Technology Corridor roadmap for 2026–2030, covering clean-energy efficiency, climate adaptation and sustainable industrial innovation.

🌿 Biodiversity

Joint Action Plan for Conservation and Sustainable Management of Biodiversity for 2026–2031.

🚢 Connectivity

Action Plan for Development of Ports and Logistics Centres for 2026–2030, including transit infrastructure and automated digital customs systems.

🔬 India-led Initiatives

SCO Civilisation Dialogue Forum, Young Scientist Forum and Young Author's Conclave were listed among Indian-led proposals.
