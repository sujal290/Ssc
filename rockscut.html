📖 Important Architecture Terms

Chaitya → Buddhist prayer / worship hall, commonly containing a stupa.
Vihara → Buddhist monastery / residence for monks.
Monolithic → Carved from a single piece / mass of rock.
Rock-cut → Structure is excavated/carved out of natural rock, not assembled block-by-block.

🚨 SSC Traps

Trap 1: Mamallapuram Shore Temple is NOT rock-cut. It is a structural temple.

Trap 2: Elephanta's exact patronage is debated; avoid assigning it with certainty to one ruler.

Trap 3: Ajanta is famous especially for Buddhist paintings; its development spans Satavahana and Vakataka phases.

Trap 4: Kailasa at Ellora is Cave 16 and is monolithic.
