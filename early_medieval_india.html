Gurjara-Pratiharas

  • Founder — Nagabhata I
  • Greatest ruler — Mihira Bhoja
  • Capital — Kannauj
  • Adi Varaha — Mihira Bhoja
  • Sulaiman praised their cavalry.
  • Nagabhata I is associated with resistance to Arab expansion.
Memory: Nagabhata = Founder • Bhoja = Adi Varaha • Kannauj = Pratihara power

Tripartite Struggle

  • Palas
  • Gurjara-Pratiharas
  • Rashtrakutas
  • Main objective — Kannauj
SSC Core: Pala + Pratihara + Rashtrakuta = Kannauj struggle.

Pala Dynasty

  • Founder — Gopala
  • Major expansion / greatest extent — Devapala
  • Important ruler — Dharmapala
  • Buddhism, especially Mahayana
  • Vikramashila — Dharmapala
  • Somapura — Dharmapala
  • Odantapuri — Gopala

Rashtrakutas

  • Founder — Dantidurga
  • Capital — Manyakheta
  • Military expansion — Govinda III
  • Scholar ruler — Amoghavarsha I
  • Kavirajamarga — Amoghavarsha I
  • Kailasa Temple, Ellora — Krishna I

Cholas

  • Founder — Vijayalaya
  • Rajaraja I — Brihadeshwara, Thanjavur
  • Rajendra I — Gangaikonda Chola; Gangaikonda Cholapuram; Srivijaya expedition
  • Uttaramerur — local self-government
  • Kudavolai — village election system
  • Parantaka I — associated with Uttaramerur inscriptions

Pallavas & Chalukyas

  • Mahendravarman I — Pallava rock-cut cave tradition
  • Narasimhavarman I (Mamalla) — Pancha Rathas; defeated Pulakeshin II; captured Vatapi
  • Vatapikonda — Narasimhavarman I
  • Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha) — Shore Temple
  • Pulakeshin II — Chalukya ruler
  • Ravikirti — Aihole Inscription
