6. MILITARY SYSTEM — REVENUE ↔ ARMY CONNECTION

→
Treasury / Assignment
→

→

Maurya

Large state-controlled standing army; infantry, cavalry, elephants, chariots and naval elements in the source framework.

Alauddin Khalji

Dagh horse branding + Chehra descriptive roll; standing army; market control helped lower army-maintenance costs.

Mughal

Mansab → Zat + Sawar. Mansab determined rank; jagir was a revenue assignment, not the same thing as mansab.

Maratha

Mobile/light cavalry, forts, Ganimi Kava, navy; Chauth/Sardeshmukhi formed important revenue claims.

Vijayanagara

Nayaka/Nayankara linked territorial/revenue assignment with military service.

British

Professional/regimental army; sepoys; post-1857 reorganisation; artillery and recruitment policy important.

Trap: Dagh + Chehra is a classic Alauddin Khalji association. Mughal administration also used inspection practices, but SSC identification commonly points to Alauddin.

42. 🚨 IMAGE-BASED SSC TRAPS FROM THE SHARED MATERIAL

Maurya: Don't memorise “Pradeshika” twice with two different functions. Terminology and translations can vary across textbooks; use the broader source-backed idea: field/local administrative-revenue-judicial machinery.
Maurya: Samaharta = revenue collection; Sannidhata = treasury/storehouse. Never swap them. fileciteturn3file4L520-L535
Chola: Don't write “province = Mandalam” and stop. The exam can ask Mandalam → Valanadu → Nadu → village institution.
Chola: Ur ≠ Sabha ≠ Nagaram: ordinary village vs Brahmadeya assembly vs urban/merchant organisation. fileciteturn3file2L227-L245
Sultanate: Your shared diagram's “Iqta → Shiq → Pargana → Village” is a useful memory ladder, but don't assume every Sultanate period used one completely fixed hierarchy.
Mughal: Subahdar and Diwan are not the same office; provincial executive/military and fiscal oversight were deliberately differentiated.
Vijayanagara: Nayaka ≠ Ayagar. Nayaka is a territorial/military chief; Ayagar refers to village official organisation.
British: Permanent, Ryotwari and Mahalwari are revenue settlement models, not three levels of administration. fileciteturn3file3L405-L453
