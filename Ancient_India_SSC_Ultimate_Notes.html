01 Stone Age / पाषाण काल

Definition: Prehistoric period when stone tools were predominantly used. The source notes describe it as ~99% of human technological history. World span is given from Lomekwi tools (~3.3 million years ago) to the Bronze Age; India is presented from Attirampakkam (~1.5 Ma) to about 1000 BCE.
Paleolithic1.5 Ma–10k BCE
Hunting-gathering • chopper • hand axe • fire
Mesolithic10k–4k BCE (India)
Microliths • bow & arrow • dog • semi-settled life
Neolithic7000–1000 BCE (India)
Polished tools • agriculture • pottery • settled life
Thomsen — 1820
Three Age System: Stone → Bronze → Iron.
Childe — 1936
“Neolithic Revolution” term.
Attirampakkam
Tamil Nadu — oldest Indian Stone Age evidence in the source notes (~1.5 million years).
Mehrgarh
Earliest agricultural settlement in the Indian subcontinent in the notes (~7000 BCE).

Core period: 1500–600 BCE in the uploaded notes. Early/Rigvedic = 1500–1000 BCE; Later Vedic = 1000–600 BCE.

Later Vedic Transformation

  • Iron (Krishna Ayas/Shyama Ayas) appears in the Later Vedic framework; Atranjikhera is highlighted around 1000 BCE.
  • Janapadas develop; Kuru-Panchala, Kosala, Videha, Kashi and Magadha appear in the source notes.
  • King becomes stronger; Ratnins and elaborate Rajasuya/Ashvamedha/Vajapeya rituals are emphasized.
  • Bhaga is described as 1/6th produce; Bali changes from voluntary gift in the Rigvedic description toward compulsory taxation.
  • PGW is the key archaeological marker for the Later Vedic period.
