The Version I Kept for Myself

Everything I couldn't put on her page, because it was never really about her reading it. It was about me finally putting it down somewhere.

🕯️

People ask me why I put you above everyone. Why I cried so much that afternoon. Why I whisper your name so often. Why I kept knocking on your door, just hoping there would still be a little space for me.

And honestly, I don't have a simple answer.

Not because you were "the one." Not because I know whether what I felt was love, attachment, or ishq. You weren't my friend, at least not in the way I could call you that. From your side, you weren't my di either. You never promised me anything. You never owed me love, attention, or a place in your life.

And maybe that's what makes it so difficult to explain.

Because somewhere along the way, I got used to your presence.

You were the person who somehow became part of my darkest phases. The person I could share things with when I didn't know where else to put them.

That's why letting you go never felt like something I could simply decide to do.

I know these things are mutual. A place in someone's life cannot be demanded. And maybe I should have understood that much earlier.

I'm an overthinker. A deep thinker. And perhaps I have always been someone who gives more than he asks for.

I kept giving pieces of myself to people, hoping that someday someone would simply stay.

I never really asked anyone for much. I just kept showing up.

People came, took what they needed, and moved on.

Maybe somewhere inside me, there was always a quiet wish: "Koi toh ho jo mere liye ruk jaaye."

I have never had a conventional heartbreak. But that doesn't mean my heart was never broken.

A long time ago, I told someone, "Tum achhi lagti ho." She replied, "You're not my type."

I only meant that I liked her presence. I never meant that liking someone automatically meant wanting a relationship. But I took those words into a loop I struggled to get out of.

I eventually did. And then, much later, I met you.

It was completely random. I wasn't looking for another attachment. I wasn't looking for a relationship. I wasn't even thinking about asking you for anything.

I simply found someone I genuinely liked talking to.

I told you that you were special to me. And when I made efforts for you, I never really considered them "efforts." Maybe because nobody had ever done those little things for me without being asked.

I never wanted to replace anyone in your life. I never wanted to become something I wasn't.

Somewhere in my mind, I always saw you as someone older, wiser, someone I could look up to. And perhaps, without realising it, I started expecting from you the same emotional safety I had always been searching for.

Then came words like — "psycho." "stalker." "obsessive." "attention seeker."

Maybe I made mistakes. Maybe I crossed boundaries I should have understood. And I accept that.

But sometimes I still wonder — did anyone ever stop and ask why I was doing so much?

Not to justify it. Just to understand it.

Because you were one of the very few people I actually shared myself with. That's why I tagged you. That's why I wanted to tell you things. That's why your reaction mattered to me.

For me, it wasn't a game.

But I understand now that something can be genuine from one person's side and still feel overwhelming from the other.

And that is something I have to accept.

I don't want to call it one-sided love anymore. I don't want to call it obsession either.

Maybe it was simply a person becoming important to another person in a way neither of them had planned.

You once asked why I made so much effort. Maybe the simplest answer is: because I was giving you something I had spent most of my life wishing someone would give me.

Care. Presence. Consistency. A little space. A little conversation. Nothing more.

I don't think I'm a person who needs constant attention. I think I'm simply a person who needs to feel that he matters somewhere. Someone who can give everything — just for five honest minutes of conversation and a genuine smile.

I'm not crazy. I'm just someone who sometimes gives people the things he himself never received.

And maybe that's why you became so important. You were there in a part of my life where I was trying to find myself again.

So, if I have to let you go now, I will. Not because what I felt was fake. But because I finally understand that someone can be precious to you without belonging to you.

You may never call me your friend. I may never be able to call you my di. Maybe there won't even be a proper word for what you meant to me.

And that's okay. Some relationships don't need a label to have mattered.

You will always be one of those rare chapters where I met a version of myself I had almost forgotten.

Thank you for being one of my safest places for a while.

And someday, when all of this is far behind me, I hope I remember you not with pain, not with regret, but simply with gratitude.

You are older than me, and life will take you wherever it is meant to. Whether you are married, about to be married, or simply living your own beautiful life — stay healthy. Stay happy. Stay exactly as you are.

And if I ever said "devotion," and you thought it was immature, maybe today I understand what I was trying to say. Devotion doesn't mean possession. It means being grateful that someone existed in your life at all.

I loved the place you held in my life.
I respect the place I hold in yours.
And now, I will learn to carry the memory
without trying to carry you.

Thank you.
For the conversations. For the moments. For the memories.

And most importantly — thank you for helping me meet myself one last time.
