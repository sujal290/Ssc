Q7 • PHYSICS • SOUND
🔊 Sound: Air → Steel
WAVE
✅ Frequency = unchanged | Speed = increases | Wavelength = increases.
v = fλ → f constant + v increases ⇒ λ increases

Why Frequency Doesn't Change

  • At a boundary between media, the source determines frequency.
  • Wave speed depends on the medium.
  • Therefore wavelength changes to satisfy v = fλ.

Sound Quick Revision

PropertyDepends on / Meaning
FrequencyNumber of vibrations per second; determines pitch.
AmplitudeRelated to loudness/intensity.
WavelengthDistance between successive corresponding points.
SpeedDepends strongly on medium and conditions.
🧠 Medium changes ≠ source changes. Frequency remains constant.

Q22 • ENVIRONMENT • CORAL
🪸 Coral Bleaching — Zooxanthellae
ECOLOGY
✅ Coral bleaching occurs when corals lose/expel their symbiotic microscopic algae called zooxanthellae.

Coral + Algae Relationship

  • Coral polyps and zooxanthellae have a symbiotic relationship.
  • Algae provide photosynthetic products/energy support; corals provide a protected habitat and materials.
  • Environmental stress, especially elevated sea temperature, can cause corals to lose zooxanthellae → bleaching.

Nearby Environment Concepts

TermMeaning
Coral bleachingLoss/expulsion of symbiotic algae; coral becomes pale.
ZooxanthellaeSymbiotic photosynthetic algae associated with corals.
Coral reefMarine ecosystem built largely by reef-forming corals.
Ocean acidificationDecrease in ocean pH linked to absorption of atmospheric CO₂.
🧠 BLEACHING → algae OUT → Zooxanthellae
